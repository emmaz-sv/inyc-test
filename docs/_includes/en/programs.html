Our Work

Programs & focus areas

INYC organizes its work around neurohealth literacy for families. Early Autism Awareness is the flagship program with live tools and workshops; adjacent focus areas guide how we grow.

Flagship

Early Autism Awareness

Bilingual screening guidance, printable pamphlets, community workshops in the DMV and China, and an interactive online screener for Chinese-speaking families.

  • 35+ local parents reached through schools and churches
  • 25 seminar attendees in China
  • 500 printed bilingual brochures; open web hub

Focus area

Family NeuroHealth Literacy

Clear, bilingual education on early brain development, developmental milestones, and when to seek evaluation, so caregivers can act with confidence rather than stigma or guesswork.

Builds on the same advisor-reviewed materials used in workshops and pamphlets.

Focus area

Youth Partnerships Across Borders

Student teammates and community partners in the US and China help host workshops, restock materials, and keep bilingual guidance available where families already gather.

Schools, churches, and rural preschool networks keep outreach going after events end.
