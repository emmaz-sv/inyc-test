Who We Are

A youth council for neurohealth literacy

The International NeuroHealth Youth Council (INYC) is a youth-led initiative based in Vienna, VA, founded by Emma Zheng with a Child Find psychologist as advisor. We help families understand early brain and developmental health, reduce stigma, and find practical next steps across language and culture.

Our focus is bilingual, evidence-informed education for Chinese-speaking communities in the United States and China. We pair clear family guidance with community workshops, printable tools, and an online hub that stays free and open. Early autism awareness is our flagship program; the broader council mission covers neurodevelopmental literacy, caregiver confidence, and youth partnership across borders.

How we work

  • Youth leadership: Students design materials, run workshops, and coordinate partners with clinical advisor review.
  • Bilingual by design: English and Mandarin resources so families can act without waiting for a perfect translation.
  • Local to global: Outreach through schools, churches, and community partners in the DMV and rural China.
  • Open access: Website, screening tool, and pamphlets remain free to download, print, and share.
